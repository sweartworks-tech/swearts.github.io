✦ The Story Behind the Art

Meet Swetha

Hi, I'm Swetha — and I make things with my hands, my heart, and a whole lot of patience. 🌸

I grew up surrounded by the colors and rhythms of Indian tradition, and somewhere along the way I fell deeply in love with turning raw materials into something that makes a home feel more alive. For me, art isn't just something you hang on a wall — it's a feeling, a memory, a piece of culture you get to carry with you every day.

I specialize in Lippan Art — an ancient folk art from Gujarat where clay and mirrors come together to create stunning textured pieces — along with Yarn Art and Madhubani-inspired designs. Every single piece is completely handmade, from the first sketch to the final mirror, just for you.
